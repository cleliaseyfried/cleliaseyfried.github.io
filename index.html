About Me

A little bit about my background, what drives me, and what I'm focused on right now.

I'm a Senior Product Manager – Tech at Amazon, now focused on Books Tech Compliance. My work sits where product, engineering, and regulation meet: I translate complex legal and regulatory requirements into clear, buildable technical roadmaps, and design systems where compliance is structural rather than bolted on.

I came to compliance through marketing technology. Over the past several years in EU Prime & Marketing, I owned measurement platforms from proof-of-concept to production, architected audience-activation systems, and led compliance-by-design infrastructure across EU privacy law — GDPR, DMA, and PSD2. That work taught me that the hardest and most durable product decisions are often the regulatory ones: I've walked away from technically superior solutions over privacy risk, and re-architected systems so that meeting the law was built into the design.

That's the throughline I bring to Books: deep experience turning regulatory constraints into product architecture, a bias for clear thinking under ambiguity, and a track record of aligning stakeholders across multiple organizations and VP teams. I studied at ESSEC Grande École and Universität Mannheim, and I care most about making complex, high-stakes decisions legible so cross-functional teams can move with confidence.
